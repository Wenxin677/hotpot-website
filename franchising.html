Franchising

Frequently Asked Questions

Short answers about opening a Pu Nha Hotpot 9999 branch — costs, timeline, training, and what you can (and can't) change about the menu.

FAQs Requirements

How do I start a franchise?

Pick a location, send our team a message with your details and budget, then we'll invite you for a tasting and a Q&A session. After due diligence on the site and area, we finalize the agreement and start the 6-8 week fit-out and training plan.

How much does it cost?

A typical single branch runs $60,000 – 85,000 all-in: $12,500 initial fee, fit-out, equipment and two months' working capital. The exact amount varies by area and space. See the full breakdown here.

I have no restaurant experience — can I still become a partner?

Yes. Two of our current branch owners started with zero kitchen hours. We train you and your first two staff hands-on at our flagship for two weeks, then stay on call for your first 90 days.

How long does opening take?

From signing to serving is usually 8–10 weeks: 4-6 weeks of renovation and equipment, 1-2 weeks of training, and a soft-opening week with our team on site.

Can I change the menu or the prices?

The core menu is fixed — that's the brand promise. You can add 2-3 local side dishes after the first 6 months with our approval. Prices for dining-in packages are set by HQ so the $9.99 promise holds everywhere.

What ongoing fees do I pay?

A 4% royalty on monthly sales and a 2% marketing contribution, paid monthly. Those cover supply-chain access, recipes, national promotions and system updates. Full fee table here.

What deliveries do I have to make weekly?

Core broths, sauces and dry goods arrive from our central kitchen three times a week. Fresh produce — greens, seafood, meat — you buy daily from approved local markets listed in your ops manual.

What if I want more than one branch?

Multi-area agreements are available for partners opening two or more branches. Each additional branch keeps a reduced initial fee of $9,000 and gets first pick on new territories — ask us directly.
