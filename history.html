Our History

From one pot and twenty stools to a chain — this is how a family hotpot stall became the Pu Nha Hotpot 9999 you eat at today. Scroll through fourteen years of broth.

History Locations
The beginning

It Started as an Argument

In 2012 the Nha family argued about one thing every Friday night: whose side of the broth the beef slices belonged to. The solution was obvious — build a table so wide, and a pot so full, that nobody ever had to share again. The first shop opened with 20 seats on Street 271 and one very tired burner.

Milestones

The Road to 9999

Every chapter below is measured in pots emptied. Hover or tap a year to see how each one began.

  1. 2012 — The First Pot

    Grandma Nha's stall opens on St 271: 20 wooden stools, 1 pot, 2 broths, and a queue of neighbors who came for dinner and stayed until closing.

  2. 2014 — "9999" Is Born

    Customers kept joking that refill number 9,999 would be free. The family printed the number on a banner — and never had to serve it, because the refills genuinely never stopped. The name stuck.

  3. 2016 — Second Branch, First Franchise

    A regular customer loved the concept so much he asked to open one himself. The family said yes — and accidentally invented the franchise program that now powers the chain.

  4. 2018 — The $9.99 Promise

    All-you-can-eat goes flat-rate: $9.99 per person, every night, every branch. Revenue doubled in six months as word spread across Phnom Penh.

  5. 2020 — Delivery & the Hard Year

    With dining rooms closed, the chain survived on family-pot delivery sets and a Telegram ordering line. Regulars ordered weekly just to keep the burners lit — and it worked.

  6. 2022 — Expanding Beyond the Capital

    The first branch outside Phnom Penh opens near Pub Street in Siem Reap, serving travelers and locals until 23:30 — the latest-closing pot in the chain.

  7. 2024 — Five Branches Strong

    The fifth branch opens, the central kitchen starts shipping broths to all locations, and the franchise program opens to serious partners nationwide.

  8. 2026 — Maybe Your City

    The next pot could boil in your neighborhood. Read the franchise requirements or start a conversation — Grandma Nha still tastes every new broth first.
