Space

  • 180–350 m² usable floor area
  • Main road frontage preferred
  • Parking for 10+ mopeds minimum
  • Ground floor strongly preferred
👥

Team

  • 1 hands-on owner-manager
  • 8–14 staff (we train the first 2)
  • Valid business & food licenses
  • Commitment to brand standards
📦

Supply & Ops

  • Order core sauces/broths from HQ
  • Buy produce from approved markets
  • Follow daily food-safety checklist
  • Use the shared POS & reporting
